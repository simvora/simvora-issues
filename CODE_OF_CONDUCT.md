# Code of conduct

This repo is for making simvora better, and it works best when everyone feels welcome to say "this is wrong".

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Someone reporting a bug is helping you, and so is the person answering it.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This latency number looks off" is great. Insults aren't.
- **No harassment**, including sexual language or imagery, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- **No spam or self-promotion.**
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post anyone's personal information, including your own.

Maintainers may edit, hide or lock comments, close issues, and block accounts that break these rules. To report a
problem privately, email [support@simvora.dev](mailto:support@simvora.dev).

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
