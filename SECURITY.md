# Security

Please **don't** report security problems in public issues, discussions or pull requests.

Email **[support@simvora.dev](mailto:support@simvora.dev)** with "Security" in the subject. Include:

- what you found and where (URL or request)
- steps to reproduce
- what an attacker could do with it

We'll acknowledge your report within 3 working days and keep you updated until it's fixed. Please give us a reasonable
chance to fix it before you share it publicly. We're happy to credit you once it's fixed.

In scope: simvora.dev, including sign-in, passkeys and progress sync.
Out of scope: denial of service and volumetric testing, spam or social engineering, and reports from automated scanners
without a demonstrated impact.
